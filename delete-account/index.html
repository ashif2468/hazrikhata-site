Delete your HazriKhata account

Remove your account and the data stored with it.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open HazriKhata and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete for ever to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email ashif.nawaz@afratech.io with the subject "Delete my HazriKhata account" and the mobile number you sign in with. We will delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: mobile number, name and sign-in sessions.
  • Any household that only you use, with all its staff records: profiles, pay rules, attendance, advances, payments, bonuses and payslips.

What is kept

  • A household you share with family members stays with them, because it is their record too. If you were its only owner, the longest-standing member becomes the owner.
  • Deleted data may remain in our database provider's backups for a short time until those backups expire.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
