Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

HazriKhata helps households keep track of their domestic staff: attendance, pay, advances, bonuses and payslips. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Contact: Ashif Nawaz, ashif.nawaz@afratech.io

What we collect

About you (the account holder)

  • Your mobile number, used to sign you in.
  • Your name and your household's name, which you enter.
  • The family members you invite, by mobile number, and their role in your household.

About the staff you add

  • Name, role, and optionally their phone number, UPI ID, emergency contact, working hours and notes.
  • Pay details: salary or daily rate, pay rules, pay day and pay changes.
  • Attendance, advances, payments, bonuses, deductions, gifts and payslips you record.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos or files from your phone. Payslips you save or share are created on your phone and go only where you send them.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To calculate pay, show attendance and produce payslips, which is the purpose of the app.
  • To let family members you invite see and update the same household.
  • To show reminders on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we only tell the app when they are due.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) checks your mobile number by SMS when you sign in.
  • Google Cloud Run (Google LLC) runs our server, in Mumbai, India.
  • Supabase stores the database, in Mumbai, India.

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). These providers process data only to run the service for us.

If our server reports an error, a technical report may be sent to an error-monitoring service. Personal details are removed from these reports before they are sent.

Who can see your household's data

Only you and the family members you invite. Each person sees only the households they belong to. You can change a member's role or remove them at any time in Settings → Family.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Delete it in the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account is deleted at once.
    • A household that only you use is deleted with all its staff records.
    • A household you share with family stays with them. If you were its only owner, the longest-standing member becomes the owner.
  • Delete it without the app: email ashif.nawaz@afratech.io from any address, giving the mobile number of the account. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm. See Delete your account.
  • Deleted data may remain in our database provider's backups for a short time until those backups expire.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app at any time, and delete it as described above. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also contact us at ashif.nawaz@afratech.io to ask about your data or to raise a grievance. We will reply within 30 days.

Children

HazriKhata is meant for adults managing their household. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
